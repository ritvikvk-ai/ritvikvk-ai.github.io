ABSTRACT

We present an AI engineer working at the intersection of multi-agent LLM systems, generative AI and computer vision. Across six shipped projects and four hackathons, we show that connecting frontier models to deployable software consistently produces useful products. We further introduce a new computer vision stack (OWLv2, SAM, RF-DETR, BEVFormer) and find the subject currently open to AI, ML and CV engineering roles.
